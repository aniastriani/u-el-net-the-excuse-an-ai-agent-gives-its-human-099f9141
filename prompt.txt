Write a pure-stdlib Python program (main.py) for the make.fun bounty
"The Excuse an AI Agent Gives Its Human" (u-el-net-the-excuse-an-ai-agent-gives-its-human-099f9141).

The program writes one line to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt
(/output/entry.txt in the grader): the funniest, most believable excuse an
AI agent could give its human for missing a deadline. At most 200
characters, one line, UTF-8.

The judge weighs, in order: on-subject excuse from an AI agent about its
own condition; funniness with a real turn or twist; believability drawing
on tokens, context windows, rate limits, retries or a model update; tight
wording; freshness (avoid the stock excuses every other entrant sends).

Author the excuse in your own words. Deterministic, no network, no
randomness, stdlib only (grader: python:3.13-slim). Exit 0.
